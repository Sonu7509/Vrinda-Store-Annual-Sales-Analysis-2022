# Vrinda Store Annual Sales Analysis 2022

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Vrinda Store's annual sales data for 2022 using Microsoft Excel.

The main objective is to understand customer purchasing behavior, analyze sales performance, and identify business opportunities to improve sales.

## Project Objectives

- Compare monthly sales and orders.
- Identify the month with the highest sales and orders.
- Compare purchases made by men and women.
- Analyze different order statuses.
- Identify the top 5 states contributing to sales.
- Analyze customer age groups and gender.
- Identify the highest-contributing sales channels.
- Find the highest-selling product categories.

## Key Business Insights

- Women customers contribute approximately 64% of purchases, compared to 36% by men.
- Maharashtra, Karnataka and Uttar Pradesh are the top 3 sales-contributing states.
- Adults aged 30–49 years contribute approximately 50% of purchases.
- Amazon contributes 35%, Myntra 23% and Flipkart 22% of total orders by channel.
- Approximately 92% of orders were successfully delivered.

## Business Recommendations

- Focus on women customers to increase sales.
- Target customers aged 30–49 years.
- Focus on Maharashtra, Karnataka and Uttar Pradesh.
- Increase promotional activities on Amazon, Flipkart and Myntra.
- Use targeted advertisements, offers and coupons to attract customers.
